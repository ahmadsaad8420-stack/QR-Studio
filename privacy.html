Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the information practices of QR Studio, a browser-based QR utility created by SAAD SHEIKH. It describes the behaviour of the application package as currently provided. The hosting provider, browser, network and third-party library providers may separately process technical information under their own policies.

2. QR content you enter

QR content is processed in your browser to create a code. This application package does not intentionally send the QR payload to a QR-generation API or application backend. You remain responsible for the information you enter and for any later action you take to copy, download, print, share or open it. A destination encoded in a QR code may collect information when you visit that destination.

3. Local browser storage

  • The app stores up to 12 recent QR entries in local storage on the browser and device where you use it.
  • Wi-Fi codes (they contain network credentials) and contact-card (vCard) codes (they contain personal details) are never saved to history.
  • Your manually selected light/dark theme preference may be stored locally. If no preference exists, the app follows your device's system appearance setting.
  • Local storage is not encrypted. Someone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to inspect it.

Use the app's Clear history control to remove saved QR history. You can also clear the site's stored data through your browser settings. Clearing browser data may also reset your theme preference.

4. Camera and selected images

The camera is requested only when you choose to start scanning. Camera access depends on browser permission and a secure context such as HTTPS or localhost. Scanning is performed in the browser using the included scanner library. The app is designed to stop camera use when scanning completes or the page becomes hidden; you can also use the Stop camera control. If you select a logo image, it is read by the browser for use in the generated QR code and is not intentionally uploaded by this application.

5. Third-party libraries and hosting

QR Studio bundles open-source libraries (QR rendering, PDF creation, ZIP packaging and camera scanning) and serves them from the same website as the application itself, so your browser does not contact any third-party content-delivery network to use it. The static hosting provider may receive and log technical connection data such as IP address, browser information, requested URLs and request timestamps under its own practices. Your QR content is never part of those requests. Review the current privacy notice of the hosting provider used for your deployment.

6. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The supplied application code does not intentionally set advertising cookies, analytics identifiers or tracking pixels. This statement does not cover technologies that may be used independently by your hosting provider, browser extensions or services added by a site operator. If analytics, advertising, forms, embedded content or other integrations are added, this policy should be updated and any legally required notice or consent should be provided.

7. Retention and deletion

Recent QR history and the manually selected theme preference remain in local browser storage until cleared by you or removed by browser/site-data cleanup. The supplied project has no application account system or server-side user database. Hosting providers may retain technical server logs according to their own retention schedules. To ask a question or submit a privacy request, email ahmad.saad.8420@gmail.com. Do not email passwords or other sensitive credentials.

8. Security and safe QR use

QR codes are not encrypted. Anyone who can scan a code may be able to read its contents. Do not encode passwords, payment card details, authentication tokens or other secrets. Check the destination and sender of a QR code before opening it. Keep your browser and device updated. No website or browser application can promise absolute security.

9. Children and legal matters

QR Studio is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to target children. Privacy rights and legal requirements vary by location. The owner will respond to genuine privacy enquiries sent to the contact email and will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. This policy is informational and is not a legal opinion or a certification of compliance.

10. Policy updates

This policy may be revised when the application, hosting arrangement, integrations or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest policy revision included in this package.
