About QR Studio

QR Studio is a browser-based workspace designed to make creating, customizing, scanning and exporting QR codes straightforward. The project focuses on useful everyday QR workflows, a clean interface and processing QR content locally in the browser wherever the included features allow.

What you can do

  • Create QR codes for websites, plain text, Wi-Fi, email, phone, SMS, vCard and WhatsApp.
  • Customize colors, gradients, patterns, export dimensions and an optional center logo.
  • Export QR codes as PNG, SVG or PDF.
  • Generate batches of QR codes and download them together as a ZIP archive.
  • Scan QR codes with a supported device camera after granting browser permission.
  • Use local recent history and choose a light or dark appearance.

How the project works

QR Studio is a static web application built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. QR generation and most QR-content handling take place in your browser. The application uses third-party browser libraries for QR rendering, PDF export, ZIP packaging and camera scanning. Your browser connects to the relevant library providers to load those resources.
